The de-identified AccessStep data package supporting this study, including the annotation codebook, aggregate class distribution, data split metadata schema, and model-performance summary, is publicly available at: https://github.com/WENQUAN0816/accessstep-dataset. Raw images from private elderly residential environments are not publicly distributed because they may reveal private indoor spaces. A DOI-bearing archive of the public data package should be deposited in Zenodo, OSF, Figshare, Mendeley Data, Harvard Dataverse, or another recognized data repository before final resubmission.
